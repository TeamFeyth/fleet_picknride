Warranty coverage

Buy With Confidence

Coverage is built around the drivetrain, the parts that cost the most to fix. Each tier adds to the one before it, so you are picking how far out from the engine you want protection to reach.

Tier 1 · Powertrain Tier 2 · Powertrain Plus Tier 3 · Comprehensive

Diagram is illustrative. Component coverage, limits, deductibles and exclusions are set by the vehicle service contract itself. Ask for the contract before you buy.

Tier 1 · Powertrain

The parts that cost the most to replace.

  • Engine, internally lubricated parts
  • Transmission and torque converter
  • Transfer case
  • Drive axle and differential
  • Seals and gaskets
  • Timing chain and cover
  • Oil pump and water pump

Tier 2 · Powertrain Plus

Everything in Tier 1, plus the following.

  • Air conditioning and heating
  • Axle shafts and CV joints
  • Cooling system and water pump
  • Fuel delivery and fuel pump
  • Alternator and starter
  • Power steering pump and rack
  • Turbocharger, where fitted

Tier 3 · Comprehensive

Everything above, plus the following.

  • Steering and suspension
  • Brake system components
  • Electrical and charging
  • High-tech and control modules
  • Emissions components
  • Instrument cluster and sensors
  • Roadside assistance and towing

Finance in your own name or under your business. Ask about titling and financing under an EIN if you are building the vehicle into company assets.

Straight answers

FAQ

Do I need a pay stub to get financed?

No, bank statements work fine. We understand not everyone gets a traditional paycheck, especially business owners and independent contractors, so we work with what you have.

What credit score do I need to get approved?

It varies by lender, but decent credit puts you in a good position. We work with several banks, so even if one program doesn't fit, another might. Give us a call and we'll see what you qualify for.

Can I get financed if I'm self-employed or a business owner?

Yes. We work with self-employed buyers regularly. Bank statements or business financials can be used in place of pay stubs, so bring what you have and we'll figure out the best path.

Can I finance a cargo van under my LLC or business name?

Yes. Bring your business documentation and we'll walk you through titling and financing the van under your company name.

Do you sell cargo vans in bulk for fleets?

Yes. If you need multiple vans for your business, we can work with our lending partners on fleet financing and help get all your vehicles on the road.

What kind of cargo vans do you have in stock?

Our inventory rotates regularly with vans built for delivery, trade work, and mobile businesses. Check our current inventory or call us and we'll help match you to the right van.

Are your cargo vans inspected before sale?

Yes, every van goes through a mechanical check before it's listed so you can drive off with confidence.

Do you offer a warranty on cargo vans?

Yes, we offer coverage options. Ask us about the plans available for your specific van.
